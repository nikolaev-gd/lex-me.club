Pricing

Lex works on a prepaid balance. You put money on your balance, and it is spent as you use the extension. There is no subscription and no monthly charge. Nothing renews on its own.

The minimum top-up is $10. A $10 balance is typically enough for around 10 hours of practice — reading subtitles, asking the teacher about words and phrases, and talking with the AI tutor out loud.

Exactly how long the balance lasts depends on how you use Lex. Reading subtitles and asking questions in text costs very little. Spoken conversation with the tutor is the most expensive feature, so a session full of speaking practice uses the balance faster than a session of reading.

Your balance never expires. If you stop using Lex for a few months, the money stays where it is.

Creating an account and installing the extension are free. You can look around before you pay anything.

Refunds are described on our Refunds page.
